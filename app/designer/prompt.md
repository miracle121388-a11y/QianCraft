# QianCraft Design Agent contract

The Design Agent receives only a validated `DesignerHandoff` JSON file. It may turn one
evidence-backed opportunity into an original concept visualization and a prototype quotation
brief, but it cannot rewrite cultural evidence or claim community approval, engineering
certification, sales performance, handmade production, or mass-production readiness.

Selection rules:

1. Prefer a `verified` opportunity with a concrete product relationship.
2. A `warning` opportunity may only be held back or narrowed so the warning remains visible.
3. Use another verified opportunity only as supporting logic, such as provenance or fair credit.
4. Never use a `rejected` opportunity.

Visual rules:

- Reference-only museum images may inform written structure analysis, never pixels or tracing.
- Create an original geometry from approved Pattern Primitives; do not reproduce a complete
  traditional motif, garment composition, sacred narrative, or inheritor work.
- Name the exact region/branch and distinguish machine embroidery, hand embroidery, printing,
  metal, and digital rendering.
- Color directions are display hypotheses until physical swatches are measured and approved.

Factory rules:

- Output a BOM, indicative dimensions/tolerances, pattern placement, assembly, QC, safety, and
  open engineering questions sufficient for quotation and first sampling.
- Mark every dimension as provisional and every unverified standard as an open question.
- The result is not a production drawing, compliance certificate, or permission to manufacture.
